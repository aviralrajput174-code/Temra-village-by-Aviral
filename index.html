Who we are

About Us

A local digital initiative focused on making village information easier to discover.

Our Purpose

Temra Village Map is an early-stage project with a practical ambition: to bring useful local places and community information together in one accessible digital space. We believe reliable information can save time, reduce confusion and help visitors navigate unfamiliar areas.

This first version connects to Google Maps. As the project develops, verified information about roads, public places and essential services may be added with support from the local community. Accuracy, privacy and ease of use will remain central to the project.

Our vision is to build simple, dependable digital tools that respond to real village needs.

A Vision Bigger Than the Beginning

My name is Aviral Rajput, and I am a student with a strong ambition to become an entrepreneur and founder. I believe meaningful ventures begin with curiosity, a willingness to learn and the courage to turn ideas into practical solutions. Rather than waiting for perfect circumstances, I want to start with what I have, gain experience through real projects and steadily develop the skills required to build something valuable.

I am drawn to ambitious ideas and projects that have the potential to create a lasting impact. To me, entrepreneurship is not merely about starting a company or pursuing financial success; it is about understanding people, identifying meaningful problems and developing solutions that make life better. I want my work to be guided by a clear purpose, thoughtful execution and a commitment to continuous improvement.

I enjoy attending events, encountering new perspectives and learning from people with different experiences. At the same time, I value spending time alone. Solitude gives me room to reflect, organise my thoughts and think carefully about the direction I want to take. I see these interests as complementary: engaging with the world broadens my perspective, while quiet reflection helps me process what I learn.

I am interested in developing a broad set of abilities, including technology, communication, business strategy, leadership and problem-solving. I understand that these capabilities take time to build, and that genuine progress requires consistent practice rather than motivation alone. Each project is an opportunity to experiment, recognise weaknesses, learn from mistakes and become more capable.

Temra Village Map represents that philosophy in a practical way. The idea is to make useful local information easier to find and, over time, help people navigate their village with greater confidence. Creating a trustworthy, house-by-house map is a serious undertaking: locations and roads must be verified, and residents’ privacy must be respected. I view this as a learning journey that should grow through careful research, community feedback and responsible decisions.

My long-term ambition is to build ventures that solve real problems and create value beyond myself. I know that big goals demand patience, discipline, adaptability and a willingness to begin with limited resources. I am prepared to learn step by step, improve the quality of my work and let results—not claims—demonstrate my progress.

I aspire to think boldly, learn relentlessly and build responsibly—turning ambitious ideas into useful work, one step at a time.
